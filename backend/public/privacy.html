1. Scope & Data Controller

This Privacy Notice explains how Azmur Group Limited ("Azmur", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information when you use the Aza POS mobile and desktop point-of-sale application (com.azapos.app) and our related online backup and synchronization services (collectively, the "Services").

By creating an account, accessing, or using the Services, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Notice.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Merchant Profile Information

  • Full name, shop or business name, phone number, role title, business address, currency preference, and optional Tax Identification Number (TIN/VAT ID).
  • Account login credentials, including phone number and password (passwords are protected using secure encryption and are never stored in plain text).
  • Staff member profiles and access permissions configured by the store owner or authorized manager.

2.2 Commercial, Transaction & Operational Records

  • Product catalog details (names, barcodes, categories, units of measure, purchase and selling prices, stock counts, expiry dates, and optional product images).
  • Point-of-sale orders, line items, discounts, tax calculations, payment methods (Cash, Card, Mobile Money, Split, Credit), receipts, returns, and held sales.
  • Supplier records, stock delivery invoices, cash register shift counts, cash movements, business expenses (including optional receipt photos), and accounting records.

2.3 Customer Information Entered by Merchants

Merchants may optionally record their buyers' names, phone numbers, purchase histories, loyalty points, and credit balances to issue receipts and manage customer credit accounts.

2.4 Technical & Security Metadata

When using online features, our servers process standard connection metadata such as IP address (used strictly to protect accounts against unauthorized login attempts and service abuse) and synchronization timestamps to keep records consistent across your devices.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide POS & Business Services: Process counter sales, calculate totals, taxes, and change, update stock levels, generate printable or digital receipts, and produce business reports.
  • Authentication & Security: Send one-time SMS verification codes during registration and password recovery, enforce staff permissions, and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Cloud Backup & Sync: Keep store records backed up and synchronized across your authorized devices.
  • Requested Merchant Summaries: Send optional daily sales summary SMS messages configured by the store owner.
  • Support: Diagnose technical issues and respond to inquiries sent to info@azmur.co.tz.

5. Merchant & End-Customer Data

When a merchant enters information about their own customers, debtors, or suppliers into Aza POS, the merchant acts as the independent Data Controller for those records and is responsible for obtaining any required consent. Azmur Group Limited processes such records solely on the merchant's behalf to deliver the POS, receipt, and ledger features.

6. Information Sharing & Third-Party Providers

Azmur Group Limited does not sell, rent, or trade personal or business data to third parties, and Aza POS does not include third-party advertising or behavioral tracking tools. Information is shared only as necessary with the following categories of recipients:

Recipient Data Shared Purpose
Authorized SMS Delivery Providers Recipient phone number and verification code or requested daily sales summary text. Delivering account verification codes and owner-requested daily sales SMS summaries.
Cloud Hosting Providers Synced store account and business records (encrypted in transit). Secure cloud storage, backup, and multi-device synchronization.
Legal Authorities Minimum information strictly required by applicable law. Compliance with valid legal process or statutory obligations.

7. Data Storage & Security

  • Offline-First Local Storage: Store records are saved locally in private device storage so operations continue reliably without internet connectivity.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the app and our cloud servers is protected with industry-standard HTTPS encryption.
  • Access Controls: Passwords are protected using secure one-way encryption, active sessions use revocable authentication credentials, and staff access is restricted by store-defined permissions.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain cloud account and synced store records for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Services and fulfill legal or accounting obligations.

  • Local Data: You may sign out, clear app storage, or uninstall Aza POS from your device at any time to remove locally stored data.
  • Cloud Account Deletion: To request permanent deletion of your cloud account and all associated store data, email info@azmur.co.tz with the subject "Account Deletion Request", your registered phone number, and your shop name. Verified requests are completed within 14 calendar days.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws (including the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022), you have the right to access and export your records (CSV/PDF), correct inaccurate information in Settings, request deletion of your account and cloud data, and opt out of optional SMS reports or notifications.

10. Children's Privacy

Aza POS is a commercial point-of-sale application intended for business owners and authorized adult staff. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact info@azmur.co.tz for prompt removal.

11. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, via an in-app notice.
