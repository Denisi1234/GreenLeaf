Azmur Group Limited — Aza POS Privacy Notice

Request Account & Data Deletion

Developer: Azmur Group Limited  ·  Application: Aza POS (com.azapos.app)  ·  Support: help@azmur.co.tz

If you use Aza POS and wish to permanently delete your cloud account and all associated store data stored on Azmur Group Limited servers, you may submit a deletion request using the form below or by emailing help@azmur.co.tz.

1. Submit a Deletion Request

Account Deletion Request

Sends your request to help@azmur.co.tz. You can also reach us on WhatsApp at +255 624 105 850.

2. What Data Is Deleted

Once your ownership of the account is verified, we permanently delete all cloud-synced records associated with your Aza POS account, including:

  • User account profile, login credentials, and authentication tokens
  • Store details, staff accounts, and role permissions
  • Product inventory, pricing, categories, and uploaded product/logo images
  • Sales orders, receipts, expenses, suppliers, cash register shifts, and accounting entries
  • Customer profiles and credit/debtor ledger records

3. Data Retention & Processing Timeline

  • Processing Time: Verified deletion requests are completed within 14 calendar days, and you will receive confirmation once finished.
  • Legal Retention: We do not retain your store data after deletion except where minimal billing or security logs are strictly required by applicable law.
  • Local On-Device Data: Because Aza POS is offline-first, you can also immediately remove all local data on your device at any time by signing out, clearing app storage in Android settings, or uninstalling Aza POS.
