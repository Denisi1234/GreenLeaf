Aza POS · Point of Sale

Privacy Policy

Developer: Azmur Group Limited  ·  App Package: com.azapos.app  ·  Last updated: November 22, 2026

Azmur Group Limited (“we”, “our”, or “us”) built the Aza POS mobile application (com.azapos.app) to help retail shops and merchants manage point-of-sale checkout, inventory, customers, suppliers, accounting, and business reports. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use Aza POS.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to provide in order to operate your store in Aza POS:

  • Account & Business Profile: Your name, phone number, email address, store name, physical address, tax ID, and staff role details.
  • Store Operations Data: Product catalog, stock levels, pricing, sales orders, receipts, expenses, suppliers, cash register shifts, and accounting entries.
  • Optional Customer Records: Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, and credit/repayment ledger balances entered by you.
  • Optional Photos & Media: Product images or business logo photos that you voluntarily select or capture.

2. Device Permissions Used

Aza POS requests device permissions only when needed for specific POS features:

  • Camera: Used strictly to scan product barcodes and optionally take product or store logo photos.
  • Bluetooth: Used strictly to discover and print receipts to paired 58mm/80mm thermal printers.
  • Contacts (Optional): Used only if you choose to import a customer contact into your store directory.
  • Notifications: Used to display local sales, low-stock, and operational alerts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Provide offline-first point-of-sale checkout, inventory tracking, customer credit ledgers, accounting, and financial reporting.
  • Sync and back up your store records across your authorized devices when cloud sync is enabled.
  • Deliver account verification codes and optional daily end-of-day sales summary SMS messages requested by you.
  • Provide technical and customer support when you contact us.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your business records are stored locally in private SQLite storage on your device so Aza POS works reliably even without internet connectivity. Authentication tokens are protected using Android Keystore hardware-backed encryption. When cloud backup and sync are active, all data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and passwords are stored using strong cryptographic hashing.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

Azmur Group Limited does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data. Aza POS contains no third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites. Information is shared only with:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Strictly to host and back up your authorized store database.
  • SMS Delivery Providers: Strictly to transmit OTP verification codes or daily sales summary SMS messages to phone numbers you configure.
  • User-Initiated Sharing: Receipts, reports, or backups that you explicitly choose to share via WhatsApp, SMS, email, or printing.

6. Data Retention, Your Rights & Account Deletion

We retain your cloud-synced store data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the service. You may export your reports and backups (PDF, CSV, JSON), update your profile, or remove local data from your device at any time.

To request permanent deletion of your Aza POS cloud account and all associated server data, please email help@azmur.co.tz from your registered email or include your registered store phone number. Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

7. Children’s Privacy

Aza POS is a business point-of-sale application intended for merchants and adult store staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to Aza POS or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
